Sir M. Visvesvaraya

Father of Modern Engineering in India

Quick Facts

Born: 1861

Died: 1962

Profession: Engineer & Statesman

Award: Bharat Ratna

About

Sir M. Visvesvaraya was a legendary Indian engineer and administrator. He played a major role in developing irrigation systems, dams, and infrastructure projects in India.

Major Contributions

  • Krishna Raja Sagar Dam project
  • Flood protection system in Hyderabad
  • Modern engineering planning in India
